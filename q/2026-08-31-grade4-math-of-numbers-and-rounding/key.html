Grade 4 Math Practice - Place Value, Forms of Numbers, and Rounding — Answer Key

Grade 4 · Math · ~25 minutes · August 31, 2026
Students use a place value chart, expanded form as a sum of place values, expanded notation as multiplication expressions, place-by-place comparison with >, <, =, and rounding by looking at the digit to the right of the target place.
1. 1) B, 2) D, 3) A, 4) C
  1. 407,050 in the place value chart: 4 hundred thousands, 0 ten thousands, 7 thousands, 0 hundreds, 5 tens, 0 ones = four hundred seven thousand, fifty (B).
  2. 470,500 = four hundred seventy thousand, five hundred (D).
  3. 47,005 = forty-seven thousand, five (A).
  4. 400,705 = four hundred thousand, seven hundred five (C).
2. C. 600,000
  1. Place the digits in the chart: 8 hundred thousands, 6 ten thousands, 3 thousands, 2 hundreds, 1 ten, 4 ones.
  2. Wait - count from the left: 8 is in the hundred thousands place and 6 is in the ten thousands place, so re-check the chart for 863,214.
  3. 863,214 has 8 hundred thousands, 6 ten thousands, 3 thousands, 2 hundreds, 1 ten, 4 ones, so the 6 means 6 x 10,000 = 60,000.
  4. Correct answer is B. 60,000.
3. B. 512,043 = 500,000 + 10,000 + 2,000 + 40 + 3
  1. Chart 512,043: 5 hundred thousands, 1 ten thousand, 2 thousands, 0 hundreds, 4 tens, 3 ones.
  2. Write each place as a place value product: 500,000 + 10,000 + 2,000 + 0 + 40 + 3.
  3. That matches choice B.
4. C. 350,000
  1. Find the ten thousands place in 348,916: the digit 4.
  2. Look one place to the right (thousands place): 8.
  3. 8 is 5 or greater, so round the 4 up to 5 and change all digits to the right to zeros: 350,000.
5. 600,000 + 20,000 + 5,000 + 300 + 8
  1. Chart 625,308: 6 hundred thousands, 2 ten thousands, 5 thousands, 3 hundreds, 0 tens, 8 ones.
  2. Write the value of each place as a sum: 600,000 + 20,000 + 5,000 + 300 + 8 (the tens place is 0, so it is left out).
6. (7 x 100,000) + (0 x 10,000) + (4 x 1,000) + (2 x 100) + (6 x 10) + (0 x 1), which can be written as (7 x 100,000) + (4 x 1,000) + (2 x 100) + (6 x 10)
  1. Chart 704,260: 7 hundred thousands, 0 ten thousands, 4 thousands, 2 hundreds, 6 tens, 0 ones.
  2. Write each digit times its place value as a multiplication expression: (7 x 100,000) + (4 x 1,000) + (2 x 100) + (6 x 10).
7. 216,090
  1. "Two hundred sixteen thousand" fills the thousands period: 216 thousands.
  2. "Ninety" means 9 tens and 0 ones, and the hundreds place is empty, so write 0 there.
  3. Standard form: 216,090.
8. a) < b) >
  1. a) Compare place by place: ten thousands 9 = 9, thousands 3 = 3, hundreds 6 < 8, so 93,682 < 93,862.
  2. b) Compare place by place: hundred thousands 5 = 5, ten thousands 7 > 0, so 570,400 > 507,400.
9. 409,732; four hundred nine thousand, seven hundred thirty-two
  1. Fill the chart: Hundred Thousands 4, Ten Thousands 0, Thousands 9, Hundreds 7, Tens 3, Ones 2.
  2. Standard form: 409,732.
  3. Read the thousands period, then the rest: four hundred nine thousand, seven hundred thirty-two.
10. Nearest hundred thousand: 600,000. Nearest thousand: 586,000.
  1. Nearest hundred thousand: the hundred thousands digit is 5; the digit to its right (ten thousands) is 8, which is 5 or more, so round up to 6 and write zeros: 600,000.
  2. Nearest thousand: the thousands digit is 6; the digit to its right (hundreds) is 4, which is less than 5, so keep the 6 and write zeros: 586,000.
11. 10 times greater
  1. The 8 in the ten thousands place has a value of 80,000.
  2. The 8 in the thousands place has a value of 8,000.
  3. 80,000 divided by 8,000 = 10, so each shift one place to the left makes the digit's value 10 times greater.
12. False
  1. The hundred thousands digit is 2; the digit to its right (ten thousands) is 5.
  2. 5 is 5 or greater, so you round up to 300,000, not down to 200,000.
13. The student who wrote 150,000 is correct.
  1. The ten thousands digit in 149,500 is 4; look at the thousands digit to its right, which is 9.
  2. 9 is 5 or greater, so the 4 rounds up to 5 and the digits to the right become zeros: 150,000.
14. Last year had more books, because 431,806 > 431,608.
  1. Compare place by place from the left: hundred thousands 4 = 4, ten thousands 3 = 3, thousands 1 = 1.
  2. Next, hundreds: 8 > 6, so 431,806 is greater than 431,608.
  3. Last year (431,806 books) had more books.
